# Mossa — Guide for Clients

Everything here describes what the app does today. Things that aren't ready yet
are listed at the end rather than hinted at.

---

## 1. Signing in

You don't have a password and you never will. Open the link your coach sent you,
type your email, and we send you a six-digit code. Enter it and you're in. Codes
last 10 minutes and work once; three wrong attempts and you'll need a fresh one.

Use **the same email address your coach set you up with** — that's what connects
you to their studio.

**Make it one tap.** After your first sign-in the app offers to save a **passkey**
— your fingerprint, face, or device PIN. Say yes and signing in becomes instant.
You can add one on each device you use, and the emailed code always stays
available as a backup, so losing a phone never locks you out.

**If your code doesn't arrive:**

- Give it a minute, then check spam and junk. Search your mail for "Mossa code".
- Make sure the address is spelled exactly right.
- You can request a new code every 30 seconds. If you're on shared gym Wi-Fi,
  that network has an hourly limit for everyone on it — try mobile data.
- If several minutes pass with nothing, tell your coach instead of tapping again.
  They can check and sort it out.

If your coach's studio is **invite-only**, only people they've added can get in.
Ask them to add your email, then sign in with exactly that address — your space
appears automatically.

---

## 2. Put it on your home screen

Open the link your coach sent, then add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share →
Add to Home Screen; Android: menu → Install app). It opens full-screen with your
studio's logo and is much faster to reach mid-session than a browser tab.

---

## 3. Getting around

Five tabs along the bottom (a side rail on a bigger screen): **Today**, **Train**,
**Eat**, **Wellness** and **Progress**. The book icon at the top opens **Explore**
— articles, routines and recipes from your coach. The lifebuoy icon runs guided
tours (the whole app, the workout player, and meal plans) using sample data, so
you can't break anything. The bell shows notifications. Your picture opens your
profile, preferences, appearance and units, notifications, passkeys, and **Plans &
access**.

---

## 4. Setting yourself up

New accounts get a short guided setup: a few things about you, where you're
starting from, how you like to train, and your food preferences. Your time zone is
detected for you. A couple of minutes, once.

---

## 5. Today

Your home screen. At the top, a progress ring and a row of metric pills for the
numbers that matter to you — tap the pencil to choose which. Below that, an agenda
for the day and a short personalised note from your coach's AI, which you can rate
with a thumbs up or down, or hide. Then a timeline of everything you've logged,
newest first, and a row of things to read. Tap any logged item to open its detail
page.

---

## 6. Logging a workout

Open **Train**. There are quick-start chips, a "this week" panel (training load
against your target, total weight lifted, active days, any personal records), and
what you've done recently.

Your coach's plan is a recommendation, not a calendar lock — if you skip a day
nothing breaks, and the app suggests whichever day you've left longest. Each
exercise shows a picture, your target for the set, and what you did last time.
Log a set in a drawer; for supersets and circuits you log a whole round at once,
with rest timers between rounds using the rest your coach set. Tap a set you've
already logged to correct it. **Repeat last** fills in exactly what you did before.

Beat a previous best and the app tells you what you beat, with an undo in case it
was a mistyped number.

**The rest timer** starts itself when you log a set and counts real time — it stays
correct even with your phone in your pocket, and tries to keep your screen awake.
It buzzes when rest is up; on iPhone there's no buzz, so glance at it.

**Swaps:** if an exercise doesn't work for you — no equipment, something hurts —
request a swap on the spot. If your coach listed an alternative it's approved
immediately; otherwise it goes to them.

You can also **browse the exercise library** at the bottom of Train, search it, and
open any exercise for instructions. Note most exercises don't have a picture yet,
the categories are limited, and there's no way to save favourites.

---

## 7. Logging food

**Eat** shows a calorie donut split by protein, carbs and fat, your net for the day
(eaten minus burned), and how that sits against your targets.

Five ways to add food:

- **Photograph your meal.** Point your camera at the plate; we suggest the foods
  and their calories and macros for you to confirm or edit.
- **Scan a barcode.** We check your studio's food list, then a global database.
- **Photograph a nutrition label.** If the barcode doesn't work, photograph the
  nutrition-facts panel and we'll read the per-serving numbers.
- **Search** your studio's foods, or the web.
- **Add it yourself** — type the numbers in, with an AI helper to estimate.

If your coach gave you a meal plan it's a **bank of options**, not a fixed menu —
pick whichever suits you and log it in one tap. Some plans allow a free meal with
a calorie ceiling. Anything logged can be edited or deleted.

---

## 8. Water, sleep, mood, fasting, supplements and labs

**Wellness** holds these. Add water in one tap with preset sizes in your units.
Log how you slept and how you're feeling. There's a fasting timer showing which
metabolic zone you've reached. Your coach's supplement plan appears as a
tap-to-tick grid, and any lab test they've requested shows up here for you to
upload — your files stay private to you and your coaching team.

**Check in** is your regular update: the fields your coach chose, optional progress
photos with their own consent tick, and space to write. One per day, and you can
fill it in up to a week late. Your coach replies and you get a notification.
Wellness also shows a recovery score with a plain-English label.

The **+ Log** button covers water, weight, body measurements, sleep, mood and your
daily check-in. Water and sleep need a number before Save will work.

---

## 9. Weight and body scans

Record your weight, body-fat percentage and tape measurements. Your studio may
also offer a **body scan** using your phone camera: you stand where the guide
shows and the app measures your silhouette **on your device**, calibrated from your
height. It talks you through it hands-free.

**Your body-scan pictures never leave your phone.** Only the measurements are
sent, and an anonymous outline is stored only if you separately agree.

Body-fat from a camera is a **range, not a single truth** — roughly within a few
percent. It's most useful as a **trend over weeks**: measure the same way each time
(same lighting, distance, clothing) and watch the direction, not the digit. It is
not a DEXA scan.

---

## 10. Progress

Charts for weight, body fat and measurements; your streak (with a one-day grace,
so one missed day doesn't wipe it); a consistency percentage; how close you've been
to your calorie target; and an activity heatmap. It opens with a plain summary
like "3 of 5 targets on track", and each metric shows whether it's in range. Every
metric has a History button for the full list. You can choose which metrics
appear in the key grid.

---

## 11. Plans, access and paying

Under your picture → **Plans & access**. See what you have and how many days are
left, browse what your coach offers, and pay by card. You're paying **your coach's
business**, not the app; your card details go straight to Stripe and are never
stored by your coach or the app. You'll see exactly what's being charged before
you confirm.

**Three ways to buy:** buy now (one payment, a fixed run of days) · subscribe
(billed monthly, cancel any time) · pay in N months (the total split, each payment
unlocking its share).

**Buying again never wastes what you have.** New days start when your current days
end — 12 days left plus 30 bought is 42. Your remaining days show at the top of
the Shop. You'll get a reminder when you're close to the end, and another if it
lapses. When access runs out your plans go quiet and you're prompted to renew —
**nothing you logged is lost**, it's all there when you come back.

Your coach can also just give you access — a trial or a comp. It works the same.

**Codes:** a **discount code** lowers the price at checkout (enter it before you
buy). An **access code** adds days for free — entered in a separate place, on
purpose, so the two don't get mixed up. If a code doesn't apply you'll get a
plain-English reason.

**Cancelling and refunds:** if your access auto-renews, the Shop says so and
offers Cancel. Cancelling stops future charges only — **you keep the access you've
already paid for** until it runs out. If a cancellation can't go through you'll see
an error and auto-renew will still show as on; try again rather than assuming it
worked. If your card fails on a renewal you'll be asked to update it, and access
keeps working until your current days run out. Refunds are handled by your coach's
business — ask them; refunding money doesn't automatically take back days.

If you buy something and access doesn't appear right away, **don't buy it again** —
tell your coach.

## 12. Using it without signal

Once the app is open, keep logging. Sets, food, water, sleep, mood, check-ins,
supplements, fasts, **weigh-ins and body measurements** are saved on your phone and
sent up automatically as soon as you have signal — useful in a basement gym. The
app tells you when you're offline and when something is waiting to sync.

Two things worth knowing: open the app *before* you head down while you still have
a bar or two of signal; and if a save looks like it didn't work, don't tap twice —
we'll show you when something is queued rather than failed.

## 13. The AI features, and their limits

- **Photo meal logging** — a picture of your plate becomes suggested entries.
- **Label reader** — photograph a nutrition panel and the numbers fill in.
- **Recipe ideas** from the foods in a planned meal.
- **Activity calorie estimate** — for when your watch didn't give a number.
- **Coach notes** — a short personal line on your screens.
- **Your progress story** — a readable recap of your last stretch.
- **Your supplement guide** — what each supplement in your stack supports.

**These are estimates, not facts, and none of it is medical advice.** It cannot
diagnose anything and should never replace your doctor.

- **Photo meal logging is a guess from a picture.** It can't see oil in the pan,
  sauce underneath, or the real plate size, and it can't weigh anything. Expect to
  correct portions — that's how it's meant to be used, and the number you enter
  always wins.
- **The label reader needs a readable photo.** Hold the panel flat, fill the frame,
  avoid glare, keep it in focus, and compare what's on screen to the panel before
  saving — a blurry shot can shift a decimal point.
- The **activity estimate** models a typical effort at your body weight. If your
  watch or chest strap gave you a number, use that.
- **Coach notes and your progress story** are written from your own logged data. If
  one reads oddly it usually means the data behind it is thin — more logging makes
  it better.
- The **supplement guide is educational only.** It explains what things are
  generally taken for; it never tells you to start, stop or change a dose. That's
  your coach's call, and your doctor's.

Writing accurate, honest check-in notes makes all of this more useful to you.

## 14. Your privacy

Your logs, measurements, photos and uploaded files are visible to **you and your
coaching team** — your coach, any coach assigned to you, and the studio owner. Not
to other clients. Not sold to anyone. Front-desk staff see your name and bookings,
not your plans or health details.

Progress photos, body scans and lab files are **never on a shareable link** —
every view is checked. Body-scan frames stay on your phone. A meal or label photo
is sent for reading **only when you ask for that reading**, and is not used to
train anything.

Your coach writes your plans, prescribes supplements and requests labs, and can
change what your package unlocks. They cannot see or change your sign-in method,
and they never see your codes or passkeys.

**Deleting your account** erases your record and everything attached — logs,
measurements, check-ins, photos, uploaded files — in every studio where you're a
client. You'll confirm with a code sent to your email, because it can't be undone.
Save anything you want to keep first. If you'd rather just stop using the app,
tell your coach instead of deleting; that leaves your history intact.

**Your coach can also remove you**, in one of two ways. If they *archive* you, you
come off their active roster but everything you logged is kept. If they *delete*
you, your record and all of it — logs, measurements, check-ins, photos, uploaded
files — is erased permanently and cannot be recovered. If you want a copy of
anything, ask before you stop training with them.

**One account, several studios.** If you train with more than one, it's the same
login, and each studio only ever sees its own record for you.

---

## 15. Making it yours

Under your picture: **Appearance & units** switches light and dark and sets your
units for weight, height, measurements, fluids, distance and energy — enter numbers
however you think about them. **Notifications** chooses what reaches you.
**Preferences** covers your training and food preferences. **Profile** holds your
details and photo.

**Accessibility.** Every button, tab and rating works with a keyboard or a switch,
and screen readers announce them by name — including the 1-to-5 mood, energy,
stress and sleep scales and which value you've picked. Turn on Reduce Motion in
your phone's settings and the app stops animating. Turn up your phone's text size
and the app follows.

---

## 16. Talking to your coach

Your check-ins are the conversation: you write, they reply, you get a
notification. Exercise-swap requests and lab uploads also reach them. There's no
chat or direct-message feature yet.

---

## 17. Not ready yet

Typing or speaking a meal in plain words ("two eggs and toast") · asking for a meal
swap or a restaurant recommendation · chat with your coach · a monthly written
recap · booking your own sessions · connecting a watch or fitness tracker · saving
favourite exercises · warmup and stretch suggestions attached to your workout · a
weekly meal planner grid and shopping list · editing or deleting an activity you
logged (food can be corrected).
